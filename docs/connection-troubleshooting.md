# Connection Troubleshooting
Please read through and try all troubleshooting steps before contacting our Support team. Alterntively, head over to our [Discord's community help channel](https://discord.gg/YjShSkcqbB) to see if another QueueSpy user may be able to assist.

## Could not verify the server. Check the address and port, then try again.
This can be a multitude of issues, please try the following steps:

- Verify the IP address and query port entered are correct;
- Your server is online;
- Your firewall has **allowed all traffic** access to your query port;
